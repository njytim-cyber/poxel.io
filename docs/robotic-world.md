# The Robotic World (new dimension)

Transcribed from a hand-drawn design page. Crossed-out words are left out.

Decisions: "enderite" in the drawing means the game's existing **Etherite**. Built in two stages: (1) portal, terrain, robot mob, taming; (2) boss and altar, obitite, laser cannon and offhand, compass deflect, jetpack.

## Getting there
- Entered by putting 4 enderite blocks (each crafted from 4 enderite) in a plus shape, with one gold block in the centre.

## Terrain and structures
- Instead of bones, there are giant broken and abandoned robots.
- Ruins, with a chest inside.
- Tungsten ore. Tungsten is used to tame robots that wander around, and to craft armour and tools.
- Oil: a liquid found in this dimension. It fuels the jetpack.

## New mob: Robot
- About 2 blocks tall. Wanders around.
- Laser cannon attack that does 3 hearts of damage.
- You can deflect the laser with a compass.
- Can be tamed using tungsten. A tamed robot defends you. At most 3 at a time.
- 2% chance to drop a laser cannon.

## Laser cannon (item)
- Crafted with tungsten and enderite.
- Can be held in the offhand.

## New boss
- Hostile, 5 blocks tall.
- Spawns with a new altar. Respawns every 30 minutes.
- Shoots lasers with its laser cannon core.
- Uses its grinder to shred players, 1 heart per second (only sometimes). Attacks normally too.
- After it's killed once, a new ore appears: obitite. The boss also has a 50% chance to drop its laser cannon core.

## Obitite
- Craft armour and tools.
- Craft a jetpack with obitite and the laser cannon core.

## Jetpack
- Fuelled by oil. Right-click oil three times with the jetpack to fill it with fuel.

## Status

**Stage 1 (done):**
- Portal both ways. Right-click the gold block; you arrive beside a return portal made of blocks that can't be mined.
- Terrain: scrap plains and mesas, rust rock, oil pools, tungsten/coal/iron ore, giant robot remains (with a chest inside), and ruins with loot.
- Smoggy dusk sky.
- Hostile robots with a 3-heart laser. They can miss, and they drop iron, sometimes tungsten or a robot eye.
- Taming with a tungsten ingot (smelted from tungsten ore). Up to 3 robots, which follow you, laser hostile mobs, come through portals with you, and are saved with your character.
